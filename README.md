# EarnVest-Teenage-Paycheck-and-Savings-Accounts
This Streamlit website allows teenage users to get a better understanding of their paycheck by inputing their hourly wage, working hours, and some more information to receive a estimate of the amount of money that will be withheld on their paycheck. Additionally, the website also teaches teens about different types of savings accounts.

## Hour Log
The Hour Log page allows for the user to enter their hourly wage and the amount of hours they work for, as well as some other info, for the length of one of their pay periods. The user is able to enter multiple jobs.

## Pay Calculator
On this page, the user can answer questions about how they are paid (the form's questions are reduced and simplified under the assumption that the user is a teenager (whose tax process is slightly simpler) and in order to find an estimate). After filling out the form, the user receives a withholding estimate, showing how much money from their gross is getting withheld every paycheck. Note: the tax estimate only applies to one state in the U.S.

## Comparison
The user can enter the individual tax amounts from their pay stub into the table on this page to compare the estimates of the website to the actual amount. It should be noted that a difference in the actual amount and the estimated amount does not necessarily mean that your employer made a mistake.

## Save Your Pay
The Save Your Pay section houses 3 tabs: 
  1. A tab where users can learn about different types of savings accounts
  2. A tab where users can input information and see how their money would grow over time
  3. A tab where users can input their bank account preferences and receive a list of options
